About this space

Suspended between the azure sky and the Mediterranean Sea, Villa Bellissima represents the pinnacle of Amalfi Coast luxury. Originally engineered into the dramatic limestone cliffs of Positano in the 1920s, this historic estate has been meticulously restored with modern architectural elegance while preserving hand-crafted Vietri ceramic tiles, original archways, and centuries-old olive gardens.

Step through monumental glass doors onto the multi-tiered travertine sun deck. Plunge into your private 14-meter heated saltwater infinity pool, contoured seamlessly into the cliff edge, where the water appears to spill straight into the Gulf of Salerno. A fragrant, private lemon grove invites peaceful morning strolls before your personal concierge prepares breakfast on the shaded sea-terrace.

The villa offers four opulent bedroom suites, each featuring en-suite spa bathrooms crafted in white Carrara marble with rainfall showers and standalone soaking tubs. A fully equipped chef's kitchen, wine cellar stocked with regional Campania vintages, and a private pathway directly to the secluded coastal cove complete this once-in-a-lifetime retreat.

Daily housekeeping and local breakfast delivery are included. Matteo & Elena’s concierge team is on call 24/7 to organize private yacht charters to Capri, private helicopter transfers, and Michelin-starred in-villa dining experiences.
