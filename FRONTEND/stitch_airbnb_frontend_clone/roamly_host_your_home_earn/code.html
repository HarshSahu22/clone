Common Questions

Your questions, answered.

Is my place right for Roamly?

expand_more
Roamly guests search for all types of spaces: spare bedrooms, suburban houses, rustic yurts, city lofts, and beachfront cottages. What guests value most is clean spaces, accurate photos, and responsive, welcoming communication.

How much does Roamly Setup cost?

expand_more
Roamly Setup is 100% free of charge. Your paired Superhost is compensated directly by Roamly to help you get started on the right foot without deducting anything from your listing payouts.

Do I have to host all the time?

expand_more
Not at all. You have full, absolute control over your calendar. You can host once a year, just during local festivals or peak ski weekends, or every week. You decide availability, minimum stay lengths, and advance notice requirements.

What help is available if a problem occurs?

expand_more
RoamCover for Hosts protects every reservation with $3M in damage protection and $1M in liability insurance. Plus, our dedicated Safety & Claims Support team is on call 24 hours a day, 365 days a year to assist you.
